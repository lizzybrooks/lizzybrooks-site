Mama Ana Kazi

Mama Ana Kazi / Mama Has a Job is a bilingual children’s book in Swahili and English, that I made in collaboration with the Tanzanian painter Bart Michoro. The book is based on my friend Lutfiya and her daughter Sarah. The book is published by Mkuki na Nyota Press, in Dar es Salaam, and available through the African Books Collective.
