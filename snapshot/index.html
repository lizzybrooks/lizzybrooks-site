Snapshot

Snapshot is a looping slide projector film that I created with the members of Southern Exposure’s Youth Advisory Board. Students in the YAB after school program staged and photographed a performance at Baker Beach, based on the short story The Shore in Alain Robbe-Grillet’s collection Snapshots. They edited the photos into a narrative series and then wrote their own versions of the event. The layered stories and images, and the materiality of the projection speak to the nuanced relationship between place, memory and the experience of an event.
